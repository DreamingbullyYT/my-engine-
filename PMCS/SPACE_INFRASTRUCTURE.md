# Space Infrastructure and Settlements

Status: planned design. Extends D-012 and D-013.

## Functional satellites and probes
Satellites are spacecraft with actual installed parts and orbits. Roles include communications, observation, navigation, weather/environment monitoring, prospecting, science, and relay. Function depends on power, batteries, antennas, line of sight, orbit, orientation, payload, bandwidth, and operational lifetime. Probes can perform flybys, orbital surveys, landings, sample collection, and remote operations when equipped.

## Modular orbital stations
Stations are large persistent structures assembled from modules and connected through compatible attachment/docking interfaces. Modules include habitats, docking ports, storage, workshops, laboratories, power generation/storage, life support, food production, recycling, refueling, communications, and shipyards. Players can dock, expand, replace, repair, and reconfigure modules.

Station modules participate in shared networks for power, fluids, atmosphere, data/communications, cargo, and crew access. Connections and capacity are explicit; incompatible or disconnected modules do not share resources automatically. Stations require attitude/orbit maintenance where appropriate, power, thermal management, and resupply.

## Mobile colony ships and megaships
Megaships are mobile settlements with population, governance, housing, life support, food/agriculture, water, power, storage, industry, recycling, docking, propulsion, and logistics as installed. They can act as planetless colonies while travelling or stationed. Their mayor can manage internal operations and plan a fleet under D-013. Propulsion and movement consume real resources and must not endanger residents through ignored acceleration, radiation, thermal, or life-support limits.

## Other settlement forms
Asteroid bases, orbital habitats, industrial platforms, shipyards, atmospheric floating habitats, and suitable moon settlements use reusable module/capability contracts but remain distinct settlement environments. Venus cloud habitats are specialized atmospheric platforms, not ordinary surface colonies. Gas giants can host orbital infrastructure and settlements on suitable moons; the gas giant itself is not treated as a normal solid-surface colony.

## Construction, docking, and maintenance
Construction requires compatible ports, robotic/crew capability, materials, power, time, and cargo handling. Replacing a module may require safely evacuating occupants and isolating power/atmosphere/fluid networks. Damage, leaks, power loss, contamination, and shortages can cause service degradation or evacuation.

## Modding
Mods can register module types, connection types, habitat environments, station services, satellite payloads, and construction recipes. The host validates capability requirements and network compatibility.
